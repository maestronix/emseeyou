## Implementation checklist

- [ ] Match the mockup's overall composition and vertical rhythm, not just its palette.
- [ ] Use a continuous horizontal timeline with entries alternating above and below the axis.
- [ ] Blend cinematic artwork into the page background with layered gradients and readable overlays.
- [ ] Use actual transparent title-logo artwork, not text approximations or poster cards in place of logos.
- [ ] Provide enough clearly labelled demo entries to exercise horizontal and vertical scrolling.
- [ ] Review desktop and mobile screenshots against the original mockup before closing the design issue.

## Artwork provenance

Third-party logo artwork must be added with its original source URL, retrieval date, rights/usage review status, and any attribution requirements recorded in [ASSET_SOURCES.md](../ASSET_SOURCES.md). A transparent PNG or SVG is preferred. Do not mark artwork as approved merely because it is publicly reachable.
